01 · How it works

No menus. No settings. No jargon.

Halo starts from the way people actually ask for help, not from a perfectly worded command.

  1. Press one key

    Ctrl Alt D from anywhere. Halo appears where you are and listens.

  2. Say what's wrong

    "The sound thing is broken." "Make the words bigger." It reads your screen to work out what you mean.

  3. It takes care of it

    It tells you what it will change, does it, and checks that it worked. Or it points at the button, if you'd rather learn.

03 · Safety

Careful by design. Enforced in code.

Letting software use your computer takes trust. These rules live in Halo's code, so the AI can't talk its way around them.

Asks before it changes anything
Every change is read back to you first, in plain words.
Never touches passwords or payments
Passwords, payments, sign-ins and permissions stay with you.
Never deletes anything for good
Nothing Halo does is permanent.
Watches for common scams
Spots well-known patterns, like fake "virus" warnings, and tells you before you act.
